# Grocery Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze grocery sales
performance across different stores, brands, cities, regions, payment
modes, and product categories.

The dashboard provides insights into sales amounts, average order
values, payment preferences, regional performance, and product
category sales across multiple grocery brands.

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### 🏪 Sales by Store and Brand

The dashboard compares total sales amounts across different store
types and brands.

Store types include:

- Value Store
- Fresh Mart
- Green Basket
- Daily Needs
- Smart Grocery

Brands analyzed include:

- Aashirvaad
- Amul
- Britannia
- Local
- Nestle
- Parle
- Tata

This helps identify the stores and brands contributing most to
overall sales.

### 🌆 Sales by City and Brand

A geographic visualization is used to analyze sales distribution
across different cities and brands.

This provides a location-based view of grocery sales performance.

### 💳 Payment Mode Analysis

Orders are analyzed according to different payment methods:

- Debit Card
- Cash
- UPI
- Credit Card

This helps understand customer payment preferences.

### 🌍 Regional Sales Analysis

Total sales are compared across different regions:

- West
- North
- Vidarbha

This provides insight into regional differences in sales performance.

### 💰 Average Order Value by Brand

The dashboard compares average order value across different brands.

This helps identify brands associated with higher-value customer
orders.

### 📦 Product Category Analysis

The dashboard analyzes total units sold across different product
categories and brands.

Categories include:

- Dairy
- Snacks
- Rice
- Beverages
- Fruits
- Other product categories

The treemap provides a visual comparison of product contribution
across different brands.
