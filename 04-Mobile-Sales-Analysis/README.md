# Mobile Sales Dashboard (2021–2024)

## 📊 Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze mobile phone
sales performance from 2021 to 2024.

The dashboard provides insights into units sold, revenue, transactions,
customer ratings, brands, mobile models, payment methods, cities, and
yearly sales trends.

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### 📱 Sales by Brand

The dashboard compares mobile sales performance across major brands:

- Apple
- Samsung
- OnePlus
- Vivo
- Xiaomi

The analysis provides a breakdown of units sold and revenue generated
by each brand.

### 💰 Revenue by Brand

Revenue is compared across different mobile brands to identify the
brands contributing the most to overall sales revenue.

### 📅 Year-wise Sales Analysis

The dashboard analyzes sales performance from 2021 to 2024, including:

- Total units sold by year
- Total revenue by year
- Total transactions by year

This helps identify changes and trends in mobile sales performance
over time.

### 💳 Payment Method Analysis

Transactions are analyzed across different payment methods:

- Cash
- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- UPI

The dashboard also compares transaction volumes across different years.

### 🏙️ City-wise Sales

The geographic visualization shows the distribution of mobile sales
across different cities in India.

This helps identify geographic patterns in mobile sales.

### ⭐ Customer Ratings

Average customer ratings are compared across mobile brands to
understand customer satisfaction levels.

### 📱 Mobile Model Analysis

The dashboard provides a detailed breakdown of units sold by brand
and mobile model.

This helps identify popular mobile models within each brand.
