# Bank Loan Applications Dashboard

## 📊 Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze bank loan
applications, funded amounts, loan performance, borrower characteristics,
loan purposes, repayment status, and application trends.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of loan applications,
funded amounts, received amounts, interest rates, debt-to-income ratios,
loan status, loan terms, employment experience, and loan purposes.

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### 💰 Loan Performance

The dashboard provides an overview of:

- Total loan applications
- Total funded amount
- Total amount received
- Average interest rate
- Average debt-to-income ratio

These metrics provide a high-level view of the overall loan portfolio.

### ✅ Loan Status Analysis

Loan applications are categorized into:

- Good Loan
- Bad Loan

The dashboard compares the proportion of loans in each category to
provide an overview of overall loan performance.

### 📈 Interest Rate by Loan Status

Average interest rates are compared across different loan statuses:

- Current
- Charged Off
- Fully Paid

This helps identify differences in interest rates across different
loan outcomes.

### 📅 Monthly Loan Application Trend

The dashboard analyzes loan applications by month to identify trends
and changes in application volume throughout the year.

This helps identify periods of higher and lower loan demand.

### 👨‍💼 Employment Experience

Loan applications are analyzed across different employment experience
levels, including:

- Less than 1 year
- 1 year
- 2 years
- 3 years
- 4 years
- 5 years
- 6 years
- 7 years
- 8 years
- 9 years
- 10+ years

This provides insight into loan application patterns across different
levels of employment experience.

### 🎯 Loan Purpose Analysis

Loan applications are categorized by purpose, including:

- Debt Consolidation
- Credit Card
- Other
- Home Improvement
- Major Purchase
- Car
- Wedding
- Medical
- Moving
- House
- Educational
- Renewable Energy

This helps identify the most common reasons for borrowing.

### 📋 Loan Term Analysis

The dashboard compares loan applications across different loan terms:

- 36 months
- 60 months

This provides insight into borrower preferences for loan duration.

### 🎓 Loan Grade Analysis

Loan applications can be filtered by loan grade:

- Grade A
- Grade B
- Grade C
- Grade D
- Grade E
- Grade F
- Grade G

This allows users to explore loan performance across different
risk grades.
