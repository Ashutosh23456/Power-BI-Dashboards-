# Vehicle Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze vehicle sales
performance across different product lines, countries, locations,
order statuses, deal sizes, and time periods.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of revenue, orders,
customers, average order value, product performance, and sales trends.

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### 🚗 Product Line Analysis

The dashboard analyzes sales performance across different vehicle
product lines:

- Classic Cars
- Motorcycles
- Planes
- Ships
- Trains
- Trucks and Buses
- Vintage Cars

This helps identify the product categories contributing most to
overall sales.

### 🌎 Sales by Location

A geographic visualization is used to analyze the distribution of
vehicle sales across different locations.

This provides a visual overview of where sales activity is concentrated.

### 🌍 Quantity Ordered by Country

The dashboard compares the quantity of vehicles ordered across
different countries to identify the markets contributing most to
overall sales volume.

### 📅 Monthly Sales Trend

Monthly sales are analyzed to identify changes in order volume
throughout the year.

This helps identify periods of higher and lower sales activity.

### 📦 Order Status Analysis

Orders are categorized according to their current status:

- Shipped
- Cancelled
- On Hold
- Resolved
- In Process
- Disputed

This provides insight into the distribution of completed, pending,
and cancelled orders.

### 🌐 Product Line Performance by Country

The dashboard compares product-line performance across different
territories:

- APAC
- EMEA
- Japan
- North America

This helps identify geographic differences in product demand.

### 💼 Sales by Deal Size

Orders are analyzed by deal size:

- Small
- Medium
- Large

This provides insight into the distribution of sales across
different order sizes.
