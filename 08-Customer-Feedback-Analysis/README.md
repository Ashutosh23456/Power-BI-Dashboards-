# Customer Feedback Dashboard – Hotel Gravition

## 📊 Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer feedback
and satisfaction for Hotel Gravition.

The dashboard provides insights into overall customer ratings, Net
Promoter Score (NPS), customer sentiment, purpose of visit, gender,
feedback categories, information sources, and feedback trends over time.

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### ⭐ Overall Customer Rating

The dashboard tracks the overall customer rating for Hotel Gravition,
providing a high-level view of customer satisfaction.

The overall rating shown on the dashboard is:

**3.60 / 5.0**

### 📊 Net Promoter Score (NPS)

The dashboard measures customer loyalty using the Net Promoter Score
framework.

Customers are categorized into:

- Promoters
- Passives
- Detractors

The NPS score shown on the dashboard is:

**9.75**

### 🎯 Purpose of Visit

Customer feedback is analyzed based on the purpose of the visit:

- Business
- Vacation
- Function
- Other

This helps identify the different customer segments visiting the hotel.

### 👥 Gender Analysis

Customer feedback is segmented by gender to understand the distribution
of male and female customers within the dataset.

### 🏨 Feedback Categories

Customer feedback is analyzed across different hotel service areas:

- Facility
- Restaurant
- Room
- Staff

This helps identify the areas receiving the most customer feedback.

### 📢 Source of Information

The dashboard analyzes how customers discovered or received information
about the hotel, including:

- Organization
- Hotel booking sites
- Word of mouth
- Search engine
- Internet advertisement
- Television advertisement
- News paper

This provides insight into the most common customer information sources.

### 📅 Source Trend Analysis

The dashboard tracks information sources over time from 2020 to 2022.

This allows changes in customer acquisition and information channels
to be identified across different years.

### 📈 NPS Category Analysis

The dashboard provides a breakdown of customers into:

- Promoters
- Passives
- Detractors

This helps evaluate overall customer loyalty and customer satisfaction.
