# 📊 Power BI Dashboards Portfolio

A collection of Power BI dashboard projects focused on data analysis,
business intelligence, data visualization, and interactive reporting.

This repository showcases different types of analytical dashboards
covering sales, finance, customer feedback, sports analytics,
workforce analytics, weather, and other business domains.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data Modeling
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Visualization
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Business Intelligence
- Business Data Storytelling

# 📊 Projects Overview

## 01. Tourist Analysis Dashboard

Analyzes tourist activity, destinations, revenue, travel purposes,
age groups, accommodation types, and trip costs.

**Key Skills:**  
Power BI • DAX • Data Visualization • Tourism Analytics

---

## 02. Tesla Sales Analytics Dashboard

Analyzes Tesla sales across different models, colors, states,
years, customer demographics, and sales channels.

**Key Skills:**  
Power BI • DAX • Sales Analysis • Automotive Analytics

---

## 03. GDP Dashboard

Analyzes global GDP data across countries and years.

The dashboard explores GDP, GDP per capita, GDP growth, global share,
and country-level economic performance.

**Key Skills:**  
Power BI • DAX • Geographic Visualization • Economic Analysis

---

## 04. Mobile Sales Dashboard

Analyzes mobile phone sales across brands, cities, payment methods,
years, and individual mobile models.

**Key Skills:**  
Power BI • DAX • Retail Analytics • Sales Analysis

---

## 05. Vehicle Sales Dashboard

Analyzes vehicle sales performance across product lines, countries,
locations, order statuses, deal sizes, and time periods.

**Key Skills:**  
Power BI • DAX • Sales Analytics • Geographic Visualization

---

## 06. Bank Loan Applications Dashboard

Analyzes bank loan applications, funded amounts, received amounts,
interest rates, debt-to-income ratios, loan status, loan terms,
employment experience, and loan purposes.

**Key Skills:**  
Power BI • DAX • Financial Analytics • KPI Analysis

---

## 07. Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard

Analyzes sales performance across outlet types, outlet sizes,
location tiers, product categories, fat content, and outlet
establishment years.

**Key Skills:**  
Power BI • DAX • Retail Analytics • Product Analysis

---

## 08. Customer Feedback Dashboard

Analyzes customer satisfaction for Hotel Gravition using ratings,
NPS, customer demographics, purpose of visit, feedback categories,
and information sources.

**Key Skills:**  
Power BI • DAX • Customer Analytics • NPS Analysis

---

## 09. Global Football Player Performance Dashboard

Analyzes football player and team performance using goals, assists,
matches, minutes played, progressive carries, progressive passes,
progressive runs, penalties, and disciplinary records.

**Key Skills:**  
Power BI • DAX • Sports Analytics • Performance Analysis

---

## 10. Production Working Hours Analysis Dashboard

Analyzes employee working hours, planned hours, overtime, shortfall,
earnings, budget allocation, city-wise overtime, gender diversity,
and division-wise work distribution.

**Key Skills:**  
Power BI • DAX • Workforce Analytics • Productivity Analysis

---

## 11. Weather & Air Quality Dashboard

Provides a visual overview of weather conditions and air quality,
including temperature forecasts, humidity, wind speed, visibility,
UV index, precipitation, sunrise/sunset, and pollutant levels.

**Key Skills:**  
Power BI • DAX • Environmental Analytics • Time-Series Analysis

---

## 12. Grocery Sales Analytics Dashboard

Analyzes grocery sales across stores, brands, cities, regions,
payment modes, product categories, and average order values.

**Key Skills:**  
Power BI • DAX • Retail Analytics • Geographic Visualization

---
